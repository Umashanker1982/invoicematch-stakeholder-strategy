# InvoiceMatch: Stakeholder Management & Productization Strategy Review

**Assessment: Stakeholder Management & Productization Strategy Review** · by **Umashanker**

## 👉 The strategy memo: [strategy_memo.md](strategy_memo.md)

(Also as a PDF: [strategy_memo.pdf](strategy_memo.pdf))

A leadership-review strategy memo (about 1,450 words) for moving **InvoiceMatch**, an AI-assisted accounts payable tool, from a promising pilot with **uneven adoption** to scalable value across the organization.

**Recommendation:** don't scale by mandate. Use a 90-day plan that fixes the trust gaps of the lagging team, gives every stakeholder an owner and a cadence, measures adoption with a scorecard, and packages InvoiceMatch as an internal product. Scale to full volume only when the day-90 gates are met.
